# Rio Classroom Refurbishment

## Purpose

The Rio classroom refurb is part of the hydropool project. Its aim is to allow **Waterbabies** (an external company) to lease out the pool and have secure, independent access outside of normal operating hours.

## Scope of Works

- Keypad-operated sliding door (secure out-of-hours access)
- Storage facilities
- Sofas / seating
- Tea and coffee making facilities

## Current Status (as of 01-04-2026)

> ⚠️ **Significant issues identified — project on hold pending remediation.**

The top **2 inches of the floor concrete** have turned to sand as a result of a **historic underfloor heating leak**. This structural defect must be resolved before any refurbishment works can proceed.

### Proposed Remediation

- Remove and relay the top 2 inches of concrete floor
- Investigation ongoing to determine the best approach
- Decision required on: **in-house vs. external contractor**

## Link to Sensory Room Project

The adjacent **Sensory Room** has been identified as requiring the same concrete floor remediation work. There is an opportunity to **combine these two projects** to reduce disruption, cost, and programme time.
