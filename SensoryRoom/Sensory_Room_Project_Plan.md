## 1. Executive Summary

Dame Hannah Rogers Trust is planning to refurbish its existing sensory room using donation funding. Three quotations have been received from **So Sensory** (the primary specialist contractor) covering sensory equipment and technology upgrades, HD projection equipment, and new floor and wall padding. An estimate has also been received for covering a Vibro Acoustic Platform (VAP), to be carried out by sub-contractor **Tugboat & Dolphins** in Plymouth.

Based on the quotations received to date, the **known project cost is approximately £16,560**. However, several areas of work (electrical, structural, decoration, project management, contingency, and VAT treatment) are not yet costed, meaning the full project cost is unknown.

This document collates all available data, maps out the proposed works, identifies the contractor and in-house effort, provides a draft project timeline, and — critically — **lists all the information gaps that must be resolved before the project can be formally approved and started.**
