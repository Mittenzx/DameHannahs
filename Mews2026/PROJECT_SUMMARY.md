# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
## Hannahwood Mews Improvements Project – 2026

**Period:** May – September/October 2026  
**Total Budget:** £50,000 (Legacy Funding)

---

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

Hannahwood Mews is undertaking a comprehensive refurbishment programme to enhance living standards, improve operational efficiency, and ensure the facility meets current health, safety, and welfare standards. The project is structured in phased stages to manage budget and resource constraints while prioritizing high-impact improvements to communal and resident spaces.

---

## STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES

1. **Enhance Quality of Life** – Modernize living and communal areas to create an improved residential environment
2. **Improve Operational Efficiency** – Optimize heating, hot water systems, and facility layout to reduce long-term costs
3. **Ensure Health & Safety Compliance** – Address infrastructure issues, emergency protocols, and environmental standards
4. **Maximize Asset Value** – Update finishes and fixtures to extend building lifespan and improve aesthetics

---
