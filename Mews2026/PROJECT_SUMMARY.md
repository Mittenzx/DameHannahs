# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
## Hannahwood Mews Improvements Project – 2026

**Period:** May – September/October 2026  
**Total Budget:** £50,000 (Legacy Funding)

---

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

Hannahwood Mews is undertaking a comprehensive refurbishment programme to enhance living standards, improve operational efficiency, and ensure the facility meets current health, safety, and welfare standards. The project is structured in phased stages to manage budget and resource constraints while prioritizing high-impact improvements to communal and resident spaces.

---

## STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES

1. **Enhance Quality of Life** – Modernize living and communal areas to create an improved residential environment
2. **Improve Operational Efficiency** – Optimize heating, hot water systems, and facility layout to reduce long-term costs
3. **Ensure Health & Safety Compliance** – Address infrastructure issues, emergency protocols, and environmental standards
4. **Maximize Asset Value** – Update finishes and fixtures to extend building lifespan and improve aesthetics

---

## PROJECT PHASES

### **Phase 1: Quick Improvements (Priority) – May 2026**
Focus on facility optimization and space reorganization to improve operational efficiency and functionality.

**Key Phase 1 Activities:**
- Strip out and refurbish disused upstairs shower room as new feed room
- Strip out current feed room at end of corridor for conversion to chair storage
- Clear alcove with computer (including CCTV) for chair storage
- Strip out red walled area next to kitchen. All units, sinks pipework and flooring to go ready for plastering and new flooring

### **Phase 2: Internal Works – May–September 2026 (Weather Dependent)**
Interior painting, decoration, and finishes during warmer months when ventilation is optimal.

### **Phase 3: External Works – Summer/Autumn 2026 (Weather Dependent)**
External painting, roofing work, and environmental improvements when weather permits.

### **Phase 4: Contingent Stage 2 – Subject to Additional Funding**
New lobby and corridor refurbishment – potential future enhancement pending budget availability.

## AGREED SCOPE OF WORKS – MAY TO OCTOBER 2026

### **Internal Refurbishment**
- **Dining Room:** Complete strip-out of existing units; wall preparation (replastering if required); new flooring; fresh decoration

- Comprehensive lighting review with LED and reactive lighting upgrades
- Removal of all plastic/foam wall and ceiling squares
- Window tinting (external view only) on new entrance route windows

- Strip out and refurbish disused upstairs shower room as new feed room
