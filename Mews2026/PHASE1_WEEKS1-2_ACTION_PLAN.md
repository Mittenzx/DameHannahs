# HANNAHWOOD MEWS IMPROVEMENTS PROJECT
## Week 1 & 2 Action Plan (18 May – 31 May 2026)

**Focus:** Phase 1 – Top 3 Key Activities

---

## OVERVIEW

This document outlines the priority actions for the first two weeks of the Hannahwood Mews Improvements Project. These three activities form the foundation of Phase 1 and must be completed or substantially progressed by end of May to maintain project timeline.

---

## WEEK 1: 18 – 24 May 2026

### **Activity 1: Strip Out and Refurbish Disused Upstairs Shower Room as New Feed Room**

**Objective:** Convert disused upstairs shower room into operational feed room

**Key Tasks:**
- [ ] Strip out all existing shower fixtures, plumbing, and tiling
- [ ] Remove and dispose of old materials
- [ ] Inspect structural condition of room and plumbing points
- [ ] Plan feed room layout and requirements (shelving, storage, work surfaces)
- [ ] Begin preparation work for new feed room installation
- [ ] Identify any electrical or plumbing upgrades required

**Resources Required:**
- Maintenance Team (internal)
- Disposal/skip hire
- Basic tools and materials

**Dependencies:** None – can proceed immediately

**Target Completion:** Structural works and strip-out complete by 24 May

---

### **Activity 2: Strip Out Current Feed Room at End of Corridor for Conversion to Chair Storage**

**Objective:** Empty and prepare existing feed room for conversion to chair storage facility

**Key Tasks:**
- [ ] Remove all feed room equipment and materials
- [ ] Inventory and relocate items to temporary storage or new upstairs feed room
- [ ] Strip out existing shelving and fixtures
- [ ] Clean and assess condition of space
- [ ] Plan storage layout for Adult Chairs and other equipment
- [ ] Identify any modifications needed for optimal chair storage

**Resources Required:**
- Maintenance Team (internal)
- Temporary storage space
- Basic tools

**Dependencies:** Some coordination with Activity 1 (feed room equipment relocation)

**Target Completion:** Room stripped out and cleared by 24 May; storage plan finalized

---

### **Activity 3: Clear Alcove with Computer (Including CCTV) for Chair Storage**

**Objective:** Repurpose alcove space for equipment/chair storage

**Key Tasks:**
- [ ] Document current computer system and CCTV setup
- [ ] Identify alternative location for computer if relocation needed
- [ ] Assess CCTV camera positioning and any coverage gaps
- [ ] Remove computer equipment and associated infrastructure (cables, etc.)
- [ ] Clear alcove completely of obstructions
- [ ] Plan storage shelving/racking for alcove
- [ ] Design layout to maximize chair storage capacity

**Resources Required:**
- Maintenance Team (internal)
- IT support (if computer relocation required)
- Storage/shelving materials (to be ordered)

**Dependencies:** May require IT consultation for computer relocation

**Target Completion:** Alcove cleared and storage plan finalized by 24 May

---

## WEEK 2: 25 – 31 May 2026

### **Activity 1: Strip Out and Refurbish Disused Upstairs Shower Room (Continued)**

**Objective:** Complete refurbishment works and prepare feed room for installation

**Key Tasks:**
- [ ] Complete all plumbing disconnections and capping
- [ ] Finalize wall and floor preparation
- [ ] Begin feed room specification finalization (shelving, worktops, storage)
- [ ] Source/order any required feed room furniture and fittings
- [ ] Plan timeline for installation phase (June onwards)

**Target Completion:** Room ready for feed room installation; orders placed for new equipment

---

### **Activity 2: Strip Out Red Walled Area Next to Kitchen**

**Objective:** Begin strip-out of red walled area ready for replastering and new flooring

**Key Tasks:**
- [ ] Strip out all existing kitchen units, sinks, and equipment in red walled area
- [ ] Remove all pipework and electrical fixtures
- [ ] Remove existing flooring
- [ ] Document any plumbing/electrical connections for future works
- [ ] Dispose of old materials
- [ ] Inspect walls and ceiling condition
- [ ] Prepare surface for replastering (Phase 2)

**Resources Required:**
- Maintenance Team (internal)
- Specialist contractor for plumbing disconnections (if required)
- Disposal/skip hire

**Dependencies:** None – can proceed alongside other activities

**Target Completion:** Complete strip-out by 31 May; ready for Phase 2 plastering

---

### **Activity 3: Full Review of Lighting and Electrical in Corridors (With Contractor Quote)**

**Objective:** Obtain professional quote for PIR LED upgrade to corridors before decoration begins

**Key Tasks:**
- [ ] Contact 2-3 electrical contractors for site survey
- [ ] Arrange site visits during week of 25-31 May
- [ ] Prepare detailed brief for contractors outlining:
  - Number of corridor lighting points
  - Current electrical capacity
  - PIR LED system requirements
  - Installation timeline preferences
- [ ] Document current lighting layout and issues
- [ ] Collect quotes from contractors
- [ ] Assess quote timeline and costs
- [ ] Present quotes to management for approval (by 31 May)

**Resources Required:**
- Maintenance Team (coordination)
- External electrical contractors (3 quotes)

**Dependencies:** CRITICAL – Must be completed before corridor decoration in Phase 2

**Target Completion:** 3 contractor quotes obtained and presented to management by 31 May

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| **Unforeseen structural issues discovered** | Allocate contingency time; communicate any delays immediately |
| **Contractor availability for electrical quote** | Contact contractors week 1; confirm availability; have backup contractors identified |
| **Material disposal delays** | Arrange skip hire in advance; confirm collection schedule |
| **Resource conflicts with other projects** | Confirm team allocation at start of week 1; escalate conflicts early |
| **Safety incidents during strip-outs** | Brief all staff on H&S; use appropriate PPE; follow safe work procedures |
