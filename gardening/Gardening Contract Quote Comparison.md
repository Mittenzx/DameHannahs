## Quote Summaries & Pros / Cons

---

### 1. Jonathan Axten – Green Green Grass

**Annual Price: £18,000.00 inclusive of VAT**
Monthly instalments of £1,500.00

#### ✅ Pros
- **Lowest overall price** – the most cost-effective option by a significant margin.
- **Existing knowledge of the site** – Jonathan has previously carried out this work and is already familiar with the grounds.
- **Fully qualified groundsman** – confirmed able to undertake all required works.
- **All machinery included** – brings his own equipment and will use the client-provided ride-on mower.
- **Public liability insurance included** – documentation available on request.
- **Flexible/surplus hours** – confirms surplus time will be used for general maintenance as directed.
- **Simple, clear pricing** – straightforward fixed monthly payment, easy to budget.

#### ❌ Cons
- **Single operative** – only one person on site; any illness, absence, or emergency may impact the maintenance schedule.
- **No formal company structure** – sole trader/small business; less resilience if personal circumstances change.
- **Limited formal detail in tender** – submission is brief and lacks a structured breakdown of experience or formal references.
- **Waste removal excluded** – client will need to arrange or agree separate disposal.
- **Fuel excluded** – additional cost to contractor which could affect willingness to attend as frequently.

---

### 2. Kane Smith – Smith's Lawn & Home Maintenance

**Annual Price: £27,000.00 (no VAT – not VAT registered)**
Monthly invoicing at £2,250.00

#### ✅ Pros
- **Not VAT registered** – price quoted is the final price (no VAT to reclaim, but straightforward if the Trust cannot reclaim VAT).
- **Detailed, professional tender** – thorough submission covering all required elements; demonstrates familiarity with the specification.
- **Fuel included** – unlike the other quotes, fuel costs are absorbed by the contractor.
- **Qualified and experienced** – self-employed professional with relevant commercial grounds maintenance background.
- **Public liability insurance included** – documentation available on request.
- **Committed to making up any lost time** – clearly addresses the risk of missed attendance due to on-call firefighting duties.
- **References available on request.**

#### ❌ Cons
- **Highest fixed price** – at £27,000 this is £9,000 more per year than the lowest quote.
- **On-call firefighter** – contractor may occasionally be called away for emergency duties, creating a risk of short-notice absence, even if time is made up later.
- **Single operative** – same single-person resilience concern as the Jonathon quote.
- **Waste removal excluded** (unless agreed separately).
- **Herbicides/materials not included** – to be priced separately, adding potential additional cost.

---

### 3. Adrian Rainbird – Sound Site Services (SSS)

**Annual Price: £24,000.00 + VAT = £28,800.00 inc VAT**
Monthly payments of £2,000.00 + VAT (£2,400.00 inc VAT)

#### ✅ Pros
- **Established commercial company** – manages approximately 200 commercial sites across the South West; strong track record.
- **Award-winning contractor** – Housing 21 'Contractor of the Year' for the last two consecutive years, with experience on comparable care/residential sites.
- **Two-person team** – sends a team of two for one day rather than one person for two days; this improves health & safety (no lone working with machinery), increases efficiency, and provides built-in cover.
- **Local company** – based just a couple of miles from the site; quick response times likely.
- **Broader service offering** – also provides landscaping, groundworks, construction, fencing, brickwork, pathways, and patios; a useful long-term relationship for wider estate maintenance.
- **Experienced, trained staff** – clearly resourced to deliver to specification.
- **Flexible and collaborative** – offers to help bring the whole site back to a high standard.

#### ❌ Cons
- **Highest overall cost** – at £28,800 inc VAT, this is the most expensive option.
- **Quote is informal** – submitted as an email rather than a structured tender document; lacks formal confirmation of all required tender elements (e.g., explicit insurance confirmation, detailed hourly breakdown).
- **Proposed staffing change** – the shift from one person for two days to two people for one day changes the working pattern; this needs to be confirmed as acceptable to the client.
- **VAT is a cost** – if the Trust cannot reclaim VAT, the effective cost is significantly higher than the ex-VAT figure.
- **Waste removal not explicitly addressed** – terms should be clarified.

**Jonathan Axten (Green Green Grass)** offers the most competitive price at £18,000 inc VAT and already knows the site, making him the lowest-risk, lowest-cost option if budget is the primary concern. However, reliance on a single individual carries operational risk.

**Kane Smith** provides a thorough and professional submission but is the most expensive option and carries the additional risk of periodic absence due to on-call firefighting commitments.

**Sound Site Services (SSS)** is the most expensive option but offers the strongest company credentials, a two-person team (improving health & safety), local presence, and the broadest capability for additional estate works. They are well-suited if a longer-term relationship with a commercial contractor is preferred.
