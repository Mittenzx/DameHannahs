# Dame Hannah's Maintenance Team — Monthly Summary
## March 2025 – February 2026

*Prepared from the Dame Hannah Maintenance Job System records.*

---

## Overview

Over the last 12 months (March 2025 – February 2026), the maintenance team logged and resolved a total of **544 jobs** across the Dame Hannah Roger's Trust estate. The vast majority of work (over 93%) was completed within the reporting period, demonstrating strong responsiveness across all locations.

## Monthly Breakdown

### March 2025 — 52 jobs | 100% completed

A busy start to the year with 52 jobs across the estate, all classed as repairs and all resolved within the month. The highest volume came from **Hannahwood Mews** (13 jobs), **Hayward** (11), and **Newberry** (10). Key themes included plumbing blockages, hoist faults, window and door handle repairs, and bedroom blinds. Notable jobs included a blocked bathroom sink at Hannahwood Mews, a broken window handle at Marchesi, and replacement of screw panels in the Physio Therapy Office.

---

### April 2025 — 41 jobs | 100% completed

A slightly quieter month, with 38 repairs and 3 requests all completed. **Hannahwood Mews** remained the most active location (15 jobs). Activity centred on hoist and mobility equipment faults (including a frayed hoist belt and a non-charging Arjo), broken call bells, curtains to be re-hung, and door issues. Works at the central Hannahwood building included a plant pot repair and a catching training room door.

---

### May 2025 — 44 jobs | 100% completed

44 jobs completed in full. Work was spread across **Arthur** (8 jobs), **Hannahwood Mews** (8), and **Hayward** (7). Hoist and equipment issues continued to feature heavily (broken Arjo wheel, hoist not charging), alongside plumbing jobs (missing bath plugs, sink drains). A carpet clean and a broken bedroom side light glass were also addressed. One platform was arranged for works at the "Other" category site.

---

### June 2025 — 38 jobs | 94.7% completed (2 carried over)

38 jobs logged, with 36 completed and 2 carried into July. The busiest location was **Newberry** (9 jobs) followed by **Hayward** (7) and **Hannahwood Mews** (6). Jobs included mounting a resident's TV to the wall, removing broken garden pots, a jammed hand dryer in the staff toilet, raised bathroom flooring, stiff bed rails, and broken door handles. A radiator was also removed and bagged for disposal.

---

### July 2025 — 65 jobs | 100% completed

The busiest month of the year, with 65 repairs all completed. **Hannahwood Mews** (16 jobs), **Hayward** (11), and **Marchesi** (8) led the way. Notable jobs included floor tiles lifting in Arthur, mould treatment in a resident's room, a broken bath controller with exposed wires at Marchesi, hoist and shower hose repairs, and a PAT test session for electrical equipment. The Ernie minibus tail-gate fault was also resolved. This surge in activity may reflect the end of the academic/care year or a seasonal increase in reporting.

---

### August 2025 — 51 jobs | 96.1% completed (2 carried over)

A solid month with 51 jobs, 49 completed. **Hannahwood Mews** was the standout location with 18 jobs — the highest single-location count in any month. Jobs included broken shower heads, hoist charging faults, a snapped plug socket at Marchesi, a stuck bathroom door at Arthur, broken door handles across Newberry, and a sink not draining at Arthur. Two jobs relating to bathroom fittings were carried over to September.

---

### September 2025 — 37 jobs | 100% completed

All 37 jobs raised in September were completed. **Hannahwood Mews** accounted for 14 jobs. Work included a broken sleep system grip, a cut rope on a hoist turntable at Hayward, a shed panel coming off, and a vehicle warning light investigation. General work also included assembling flat-pack furniture in the foyer and a rear door repair at the Charity Shop in Saltash. All hoist and equipment jobs were resolved.

---

### October 2025 — 41 jobs | 95.1% completed (2 carried over)

41 jobs including 2 projects — fitting keypad combination locks on the Speech and Language Therapy Office doors. **Hannahwood Mews** (15 jobs) and **Marchesi** (8) were the busiest areas. Key jobs included hoist button faults, a window blind that had come off the wall, a blocked bathroom sink, a loo roll holder refit, and fire door checks following a fire drill. Seasonal work began with hooks being fitted in Arthur for Christmas lights.

---

### November 2025 — 37 jobs | 91.9% completed (3 carried over)

37 jobs with 34 completed by month end. Activity was more evenly spread, with **Marchesi** (7), **Gilleys** (6), and **Hannahwood Mews** (6) leading. Electrical issues were prominent this month — non-working lights in Day Services, bathroom lights, medicine cupboard lights, and kitchen lights. Plumbing work included a leaking washing machine at Marchesi and a blocked drain. A boiler low-pressure alert at Gilleys was also attended to. The Ernie minibus developed a door seal leak.

---

### December 2025 — 32 jobs | 100% completed

The quietest month of the year, reflecting the holiday period, but all 32 jobs raised were fully completed. **Arthur** was unusually busy (10 jobs), with multiple jobs raised on Christmas Eve alone — including a broken drawer, a broken wardrobe shelf, a flashing bathroom light, a staff toilet door dragging on the floor, and an oven not working. A leaking shower head and a faulty vehicle engine management light were also resolved. Light bulb replacements and a sleep system chair belt adjustment featured across other locations.

---

### January 2026 — 61 jobs | 91.8% completed (5 carried over)

The new year brought a significant upturn in job volume — 61 jobs, the second busiest month of the year. **Hannahwood Mews** had 18 jobs, with **Newberry** (12) and **Gilleys** (7) also busy. Key work included multiple hoist faults, trip hazards in the kitchen flooring, a spotty conservatory light, a broken feed room door handle, a blocked washbasin, and a fallen windowsill. Gutter blockages at Newberry were investigated. Five jobs (including a gutter repair and a bedroom light switch) remained in progress at month end.

---

### February 2026 — 45 jobs | 93.3% completed (3 ongoing)

45 jobs logged, 42 completed. **Hannahwood Mews** (15 jobs), **Arthur** (14), and **Hayward** (6) were most active. Notable work included a hoist fault in Hayward's lounge, a noisy tumble dryer and ceiling tile replacement at Hannahwood Mews, a shower cable relocation at Arthur, a fridge bulb replacement, and noisy pipes in the Finance Office. A window reflective film was ordered (awaiting parts) and several new jobs are currently being assessed as of the report date.

## Key Observations

1. **Hoist and mobility equipment is the single biggest recurring maintenance need.** Almost every month features multiple hoist faults — buttons not working, hoists not charging, tracking issues. These are critical for resident care and consistently form the top priority jobs.

2. **Hannahwood Mews generates more maintenance requests than any other building**, accounting for approximately 28% of all jobs in the year. The size and resident dependency level of this facility drives a high volume of daily-use equipment and building repairs.

3. **Electrical and lighting faults are frequent but generally quick to resolve.** Light bulbs, sockets, and fittings account for around 1 in 7 jobs and rarely require specialist contractors.

4. **Plumbing problems — particularly washing machines, shower heads, and blocked drains — are consistent throughout the year.** These recur regularly across most locations and represent a significant proportion of reactive work.

5. **Winter months (December–January) see a spike in heating, boiler, and structural issues**, while summer months (July–August) see higher overall volume — possibly reflecting higher reporting rates when care staffing levels are at their peak.

6. **The completion rate is excellent.** Over the 12-month period, the vast majority of jobs are completed promptly, with very few remaining open beyond a few weeks. Outstanding jobs are typically those awaiting specialist contractors or replacement parts.
