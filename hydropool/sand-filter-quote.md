# Sand Filters & Pool Pump Alterations – ISCA Quote

## Sand Filters

Our proposal includes the removal and disposal of the existing sand filter and filter media, followed by the supply and installation of **3 smaller sand filters**, each complete with:

- New filter media
- Multi-port valves
- Top mount pressure gauges
- Side service hatches

## Pool Pump Alterations

In addition to the new sand filters, we also need to supply and install:

- A suitably sized **variable speed drive** to the existing pool pump
- A **flow meter** to the existing pool pump

This will enable precise adjustment of pump speed to ensure optimal backwashing performance across each filter.
